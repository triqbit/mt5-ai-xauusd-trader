## 2025-05-15 - [Zero-Dependency Institutional UX]
**Learning:** UX improvements in enterprise trading systems don't always require heavy UI libraries. A simple character-based banner and actionable rejection messages (providing specific thresholds and current values) significantly improve operator confidence and reduce troubleshooting time. Zero-dependency enhancements ensure stability and portability across resource-constrained or highly secure environments.
**Action:** Prioritize actionable error messages that include the "why" and "what threshold was hit" to improve system transparency.
