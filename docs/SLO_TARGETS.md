# Service Level Objectives (SLO) & Reliability Standards

This document defines the measurable reliability standards for the MT5 AI/ML Trading Bot repository and runtime system. These objectives turn "enterprise quality" into trackable metrics.

## 1. System Availability (Uptime)

Uptime is defined as the percentage of time the trading bot is operational and able to process market data or execute trades during active market hours.

| Mode | Target Uptime | Monthly Error Budget |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LIVE Trading** | **99.99%** | **4.38 minutes** |
| **DEMO Trading** | **99.50%** | **3.65 hours** |
| **Backtesting** | 99.00% | 7.30 hours |

*Note: Market hours for XAUUSD typically exclude weekends.*

## 2. CI/CD Pipeline Performance

To ensure repository health and development velocity, the CI pipeline must be stable and efficient.

- **Success Rate Target:** **95%** of all commits to `main` and `develop` branches must pass all status checks on the first attempt.
- **Build Duration Target:** < 10 minutes for full CI suite (linting, types, unit tests).
- **Deployment Success Rate:** 100% success for automated deployments to staging/production environments.

## 3. Incident Response SLAs

Response times for automated alerts generated by the monitoring system.

| Severity | Definition | Response Time (SLA) | Escalation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1 - Critical** | Trading Halted / Fatal Error | **< 5 Minutes** | Immediate |
| **P2 - High** | Degraded Performance / Risk Limit Near | **< 30 Minutes** | 1 Hour |
| **P3 - Medium** | Monitoring/Data Quality Issues | **< 2 Hours** | 24 Hours |
| **P4 - Low** | Informational / Optimization Suggestion | **< 24 Hours** | N/A |

## 4. Runtime Latency Targets

Latency is critical for execution quality and model performance.

### 4.1 Model Inference Latency
Measured from feature vector readiness to prediction output.

- **P50 (Median):** **< 10ms**
- **P95:** **< 25ms**
- **P99:** **< 50ms**

### 4.2 Order Execution Latency
Measured from signal generation to broker acknowledgment.

- **Target:** **< 100ms** (p95)

## 5. Backtest Performance

Targets for the backtesting engine to ensure rapid strategy iteration.

- **Standard Backtest:** **< 30 minutes** (1 month of M1 data with 140+ features).
- **Full Historical Run:** **< 4 hours** (1 year of M5 data).

## 6. Disaster Recovery & Continuity

Recovery targets in the event of a catastrophic system failure.

- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO):** **< 30 minutes** (Time to return to operational status).
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO):** **< 1 hour** (Maximum allowable data loss/loss of state).

## 7. Error Budget Framework

The Error Budget is the maximum amount of time the system can be failing without violating the SLO.

### 7.1 Budget Calculation (99.99% Uptime)
- **Monthly:** 4 minutes 23 seconds
- **Weekly:** 1 minute 1 second
- **Daily:** 8.6 seconds

### 7.2 Budget Policy
- **Green (>20% budget remaining):** Normal operations and feature development.
- **Yellow (10-20% budget remaining):** Prioritize reliability tasks; review recent incidents.
- **Red (<10% budget remaining):** **Feature Freeze.** All engineering resources pivot to stability and reliability improvements until the budget is restored.

---

## 8. Measurement & Reporting

- **Monitoring Tool:** Prometheus & Grafana.
- **Reporting Frequency:** Monthly Reliability Review.
- **Audit Trail:** All SLO violations must have a corresponding Incident Post-Mortem.
