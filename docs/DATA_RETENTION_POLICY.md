# Data Retention Policy

## 1. Overview
This policy defines the retention periods for operational data generated by the MT5 AI/ML Trading Bot. It ensures compliance with regulatory requirements while optimizing storage and system performance.

## 2. Retention Windows

| Data Type | Retention Period | Justification | Action after Expiry |
|-----------|------------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Application Logs | 90 Days | Troubleshooting and operational monitoring. | Automated Purge |
| Trade Records | 7 Years | Financial regulatory compliance (e.g., MiFID II, SEC). | Archive then Purge |
| Risk Events | 7 Years | Audit trail for circuit breakers and risk rejections. | Archive then Purge |
| Model Signals | 1 Year | Performance analysis and model retraining. | Purge |
| Performance Metrics | 7 Years | Long-term performance tracking and reporting. | Archive then Purge |
| Backtest Results | 2 Years | Strategy validation and historical comparison. | Purge |

## 3. Archival Rules
- **Trade Records and Risk Events**: Before purging from the live database, data must be exported to a compressed format (e.g., CSV or Parquet) and stored in secure, long-term storage (e.g., AWS S3 Glacier).
- **Performance Metrics**: Annual summaries must be preserved indefinitely, while granular daily/intraday metrics follow the 7-year retention rule.

## 4. Automated Purging
The `scripts/data_cleanup.py` script is scheduled to run monthly to enforce these retention windows.
- **Database**: Records older than the specified window based on `created_at` or `timestamp` columns are removed.
- **Logs**: Files in the `logs/` directory older than 90 days are deleted.

## 5. Auditability vs. Storage Control
- **Preserve for Auditability**: Ticket numbers, entry/exit prices, timestamps, risk approval IDs, and reason codes for circuit breaker activations.
- **Rotate for Storage Control**: Verbose debug logs, intermediate model calculation states, and non-essential metadata.

## 6. Compliance
This policy is reviewed annually to ensure alignment with changing financial regulations and technical requirements.
